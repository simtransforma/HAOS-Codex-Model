# Templates de Apelação Meta

## TEMPLATE 1: UNUSUAL ACTIVITY (uso geral)

### Versão Inglês

```
Hello Meta Review Team,

We are [COMPANY NAME], a [COUNTRY] education company operating legally with a registered [TAX ID TYPE]. We produce content and courses focused on [NICHE], and we have been advertising on Meta platforms in full alignment with the Advertising Standards and Community Standards. Our team manages campaigns through a verified Business Manager with proper admin controls and 2FA enabled.

On [DATE], the ad account [ACCOUNT ID] was disabled for "unusual activity." We believe this was triggered by [SPECIFIC TRIGGER, e.g., the activation of a new Click to Messenger campaign integrated with ManyChat, an official Meta Business Partner]. Since this Business Manager is recent and this was [CONTEXT, e.g., its first MESSAGES objective campaign], the activity pattern may have looked atypical, although no policy was knowingly violated.

We respectfully request a manual review and reinstatement of the account. We are fully committed to Meta's Advertising Policies, rate limits, and Account Integrity standards, and remain available to provide any documentation needed.

Thank you for your time.
```

## TEMPLATE 4: SEGUNDA APELAÇÃO (após negação)

### Versão Inglês

```
Hello Meta Review Team,

Following our previous request for review of ad account [ID], we would like to provide additional context.

We have confirmed with ManyChat (Meta Business Partner) that our integration is legitimate. Their support ticket [NUMBER] confirms the campaign setup complies with their best practices and Meta's CTM ad guidelines.

We have also reviewed and updated:
- 2FA on all admin accounts
- System User configured for ManyChat integration (not personal admin)
- Verified domain in Business Manager
- Rate limits on ManyChat flows adjusted to 60s+ between messages

We respectfully request a new review with this updated context.

Thank you.
```

## REGRAS DE OURO DOS TEMPLATES

1. **Tamanho:** 130-170 palavras MÁXIMO
2. **3 parágrafos:** legitimidade + fato + compliance
3. **Zero "—" (em-dash)**
4. **Zero vitimismo**
5. **Zero acusação**
6. **Mencionar conhecimento das políticas** (Advertising Standards, Account Integrity)
7. **Disponibilizar documentação SE pedirem** (não anexar antes)
8. **Inglês como principal** (fila internacional mais rápida)
9. **PT-BR como backup**
10. **Salvar print do texto enviado** com timestamp

## VARIÁVEIS PARA PREENCHER

Antes de enviar QUALQUER template, substituir:
- `[COMPANY NAME]` / `[NOME EMPRESA]`
- `[COUNTRY]` / país
- `[TAX ID TYPE]` (CNPJ no Brasil)
- `[NICHE]` / `[NICHO]`
- `[DATE]` / `[DATA]`
- `[ACCOUNT ID]` / `[ID]`
- `[SPECIFIC TRIGGER]` / `[GATILHO ESPECÍFICO]`
- `[CONTEXT]` / `[CONTEXTO]`

## CHECKLIST PRÉ-ENVIO

- [ ] Texto entre 130-170 palavras
- [ ] Zero travessão "—"
- [ ] Zero vitimismo/acusação
- [ ] Variáveis substituídas
- [ ] Idioma adequado (EN principal, PT-BR backup)
- [ ] Print salvo com timestamp
- [ ] 2FA confirmado em todos os admins
- [ ] Documentação separada em pasta (CNPJ, contrato, RG, etc)
- [ ] NÃO criar BM nova em paralelo
- [ ] Aguardar 72h antes de qualquer segunda ação
